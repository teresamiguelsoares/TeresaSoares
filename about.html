About

Barcelona now, Lisbon from February

I’m Teresa, a multidisciplinary designer who likes pages you can touch and screens that move. I start from the text and let the form follow, whether it ends up as a book, a brand or a website.

I’m looking for work in graphic, editorial and branding design, in Lisbon from February.

I can’t sit still: I’m always learning something new, especially by hand, from crochet and embroidery to digital drawing and 3D. There’s music on while I work, mostly pop and indie, and I love concerts, travelling, taking photos and being with my friends.

I work in Portuguese, English and Spanish, with InDesign, Illustrator, Photoshop, Figma, Procreate, Blender and a bit of code.
